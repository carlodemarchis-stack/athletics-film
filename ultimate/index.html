Ultimate Championship

Budapest 2026 · A guy with a scarf

One card per event, in the order they are contested. A card that has been run opens on its final — the podium, the finishing order, and the race replayed underneath. Every finisher moves at their own average pace, so when the winner crosses, the field freezes and the view closes in on the line: the gaps are then read in metres, not hundredths.

Semifinals sit behind the tabs. Events still to come show their schedule — World Athletics publishes no start list anywhere public, so the field is not known here until it runs.
